    Product details

    Blue-and-white, focused, and intentionally simple.

    The design keeps Todoist-like clarity while avoiding extra concepts: no projects, no labels, no accounts. It is built around the three promised actions: add, complete/uncomplete, and delete.

    The todo card becomes full-width on mobile, navigation collapses into a menu, and task controls stay touch-friendly.

    The preview uses browser state only, but the production flow is designed for create, read, update completion, and delete operations backed by the database.

    Animations are short and functional: card entrance, task insertion, completion fade, and subtle button lift.
